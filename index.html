Why Jakten Risk?

Most project teams understand contingency is needed. Few can clearly explain why.

Jakten Risk combines estimate uncertainty, dependency modeling, risk attribution, and Monte Carlo simulation into a practical workflow built specifically for EPC and construction projects.

Built for practical project risk analysis

Monte Carlo simulation

Run thousands of iterations to quantify cost uncertainty and funding percentiles.

Risk register modeling

Capture discrete risks alongside estimate uncertainty in one workspace.

Correlation and dependencies

Model how risk drivers move together instead of treating them as independent.

Scenario management

Compare funding outcomes across alternative project assumptions.

Funding recommendation

Translate simulation results into a clear, defensible funding number.

Contingency attribution

Show which risks and uncertainties drive contingency requirements.

Executive PDF reporting

Generate stakeholder-ready reports without rebuilding slides.

CSV export

Export results for analysis in Excel or downstream systems.
