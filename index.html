Why Jakten Risk?

When leadership asks “why this contingency?”, Jakten Risk gives your team an answer they can defend.

Most project teams know contingency is required. Few can explain the drivers clearly. Jakten Risk is purpose-built risk analysis for construction — not generic finance software — combining structured risk registers, dependency modeling, Monte Carlo simulation, and contingency attribution in one practical workflow.

What Jakten Risk does

Monte Carlo risk analysis for construction estimates — from structured inputs to funding recommendations your leadership can review.

  • Build a structured risk register
  • Model probability and cost impact
  • Run Monte Carlo simulations
  • Account for correlation and dependencies
  • Determine contingency
  • Explain contingency attribution
  • Export clear executive summaries
